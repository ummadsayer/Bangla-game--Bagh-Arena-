Bagh Arena (বাঘ অ্যারেনা): a free Bangla 3D shooter

Bagh Arena is a free first-person shooter that runs in your web browser at bagh.undo.it. It is set in Bangladesh: the lanes of Puran Dhaka, a village with paddy fields and a pond, the misty Sundarbans, and a lively Boishakhi Mela with a giant Nagordola wheel. Battle clever bots in Free for All, Team Battle or the Gun Rush (অস্ত্র দৌড়) mode, where every two kills hand you a new weapon.

বাঘ অ্যারেনা একটি বিনামূল্যের থ্রিডি শুটার গেম, যা সরাসরি ব্রাউজারে খেলা যায়। পুরান ঢাকা, গ্রাম বাংলা, সুন্দরবন আর বৈশাখী মেলার মানচিত্রে বটদের সাথে লড়ো। কোনো বিজ্ঞাপন নেই, কোনো ডাউনলোড নেই, ফোন আর কম্পিউটার দুটোতেই চলে।

Rooted in Bengal

Weapons are named after Bangladeshi rivers (Padma, Meghna, Jamuna, Karnaphuli). Skins are inspired by Nakshi Kantha, Alpona, rickshaw art, Gamchha, Jamdani and the Shapla. Pickups are mishti sweets, chai and jhalmuri, the menu music is a Bhupali raga with tabla, and the game shows today's Bangla date and season.

Play on phone or computer

On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen joystick, drag to look, and tap fire. Push the joystick to its edge to run. Aim assist and auto-fire help on touch screens, and everything can be tuned in Settings. On a computer, use the keyboard and mouse: W A S D to move, Shift to run, C to slide, mouse to aim and shoot. Your name, level, coins, unlocks, badges and settings are saved in your browser on your own device, so you can close the page and continue later. You can also install it like an app and play offline.

Is Bagh Arena free?

Yes. It is free to play with no ads, no purchases and no random loot boxes. Coins and cosmetics are earned only by playing.

Who made it?

Bagh Arena was created by Ahnaf T. and is dedicated to Osman Goni and Abrar Fahad (ওসমান গনি ও আবরার ফাহাদ).

Does it work offline?

After the first visit the game can be installed and opened without internet. Matches are against bots, so no connection is needed to play.
